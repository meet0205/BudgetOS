Desktop wireframes

Left navigation, period controls, metric cards, category donut, income-to-allocation Sankey, upcoming bills, category trend

06 — Tax settings, business use, HST & onboarding
Province and bracket freshness, manual odometer and CCA, HST as separate obligation, first-run flow
Decisions these wireframes encode

Import is the primary intake, not camera. Desktop-first means receipts arrive as files. The queue table with per-file status and bulk accept-above-threshold is what makes 30 files tolerable.

The column gate shows its evidence. Rather than just asking which column is the pay period, it shows the 14× ratio, the exact-balance check on both, and the cross-check against the previous stub. Evidence makes a hard stop feel reasonable rather than obstructive.

Tax reserve is visually distinct everywhere. Never merged into safe-to-spend. It appears on its own tint, with its own bucket row, locked.

CPP self-employed sits on a warning tint. It's the line most likely to surprise, and burying it in a total is how people get caught out in April.

Numbers shown are illustrative
Every dollar figure, bracket, and rate in these wireframes is placeholder data for layout purposes. Real CRA values — federal and Nova Scotia brackets, basic personal amounts, CPP/EI rates and maximums, CPP2, the instalment threshold — must be populated from CRA sources before feature 05 is built.
